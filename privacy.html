Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and how we protect it when you visit our sales page, buy the Financial Security Guide, the Financial Security System or WealthMe, or join our email list.

“We”, “us” and “our” refer to Priscila Veloso, an independent creator based in Canada. We do not sell your personal information.

Effective date: November 6, 2026. Questions? Write to priscila.support@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to deliver your purchase, support you and run our business.

  • When you buy: our checkout is run by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email, billing address and payment details. We receive your name, email, country and order details. We never see or store your full card details.
  • When you join our email list: your name and email address, collected through MailerLite.
  • When you contact us: your email address and anything you include in your message.
  • When you visit our sales page: technical information such as your IP address, browser, device type and the pages you view, collected through cookies and similar tools used by MailerLite and our advertising partners (see Third-party services).

Your financial data in WealthMe

The financial information you enter in WealthMe stays on your own device. It is saved only in your browser and is never sent to us or to any server, so we cannot see, access or recover it.

  • License key check: to activate WealthMe, the app verifies your license key with Lemon Squeezy. This check sends your license key and a device identifier, never your financial data.
  • No sync between devices: your license key works on up to 3 devices, and each device keeps its own data. A new device starts empty.
  • Export and import: you can export your data to a file and import it on another device. That file contains your financial information, so keep it somewhere safe and delete copies you no longer need.
  • Back up your data: clearing your browser data or uninstalling your browser can erase your WealthMe data. Because we never hold a copy, we cannot restore it. Export a backup regularly.

How we use your information

  • Deliver your purchase, including your guide, license key and app access
  • Send transactional emails, such as receipts and access instructions
  • Answer your questions and provide support
  • Send marketing emails, only if you subscribed to them
  • Understand how our sales page performs and measure our ads
  • Meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations

Emails and unsubscribing

We send marketing emails only to people who subscribed to them. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can leave at any time.

Even after you unsubscribe, we may still send emails about your purchase, such as receipts, access details or important updates to a product you bought.

How long we keep it and how we protect it

We keep order information for as long as we need it to support your purchase and to meet tax and legal requirements. We keep your email on our list until you unsubscribe, and support messages only as long as they are useful to help you.

We use reputable service providers and reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No online service can promise perfect security, but we never hold your WealthMe financial data, so it cannot be exposed through us.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to marketing emails. Write to priscila.support@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also object to or restrict how we use your information, ask for a copy in a portable format, and complain to your local data protection authority.

Payment and billing details are held by Lemon Squeezy. For those, we will help you, and you can also contact Lemon Squeezy directly.

Other important details

  • Children: our products are not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
  • International transfers: our service providers may store information outside Canada, including in the United States and the European Union, under their own safeguards.
  • Changes to this policy: if we update this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
